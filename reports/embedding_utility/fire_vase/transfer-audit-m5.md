# Audit of the original four-dimensional representation

The M4 protocol/configuration, frozen source cohort, neural implementation, raw and
interpretable/PCA baselines, perturbations, materialization ledger, utility framework
and unsupported claims were reviewed before the new task evaluation.

| Layer | Established | Discarded or unknown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Input tensor | All supplied prefix increments, cumulative prefix sums, mask and observed length; train-only normalization invertible within floating precision | No future observation, completed fire endpoint, climate or geometry; IDs/dates remain in source lineage, not numeric model inputs |
| A-supervised encoder | Fixed weights learned only from training next-day log-growth targets; M4 showed approximate held-out predictive utility | Four numbers are not an exact general prefix inverse; which specific properties survive is not established by dimension or one prediction |
| Prediction head | Original nonlinear training head and a new linear probe recover next-day signal approximately | Failed linear prediction does not prove information is absent; a different head may recover other information |
| Other tasks | No original independent evidence of B–E utility | General scientific relevance, ecological analogs, exact property preservation and real transfer are unknown |

M4 learned4 ridge did not beat raw ridge, and similar-capacity tabular/temporal networks
performed similarly. This neither proves embeddings useless for other tasks nor
justifies generality. A's target is correlated with the new tasks; positive transfer
could reflect shared statistical structure, not general scientific understanding.
The cohort's A test performance was previously visible. B–E use a new frozen protocol
on existing histories, not a new independently hidden dataset.

Source/provenance sidecars count toward the full interface: numeric embeddings omit
native IDs/dates, but retained lineage still identifies the supplied observations.
Do not claim lost provenance or missingness knowledge merely because it is not in
four latent coordinates. Conversely, known source completeness does not establish
that an embedding learned to detect missing observations. Invalid schedules are
rejected before encoding; all accepted histories have complete daily support.

The new experiment separates frozen A-only transfer, added multitask supervision,
separate task-specific retraining, downstream head capacity and storage reuse. Exact
mathematical facts, synthetic observations, future-outcome relevance, unmeasured
lifecycle costs and unsupported real-world claims remain distinct.
