# Claims not supported by Milestone 4

- Real agents benefit from scientific metadata or adaptive representation access.
- A real-model smoke/pilot executed, or usage/cost was measured.
- The learned representation contains new observations beyond the same raw prefix.
- Learned embeddings uniquely explain the observed predictive differences rather
  than supervised training, capacity, regularization or inductive bias.
- The learned frozen probe improves on strong raw-prefix ridge in this run.
- Predictive skill generalizes to real fires, years, geographic regions or sensors.
- Synthetic region/year labels test ecological transfer or distribution shift.
- Reconstructed source dates prove operational availability of real observations.
- Geometric neighborhood overlap establishes ecological analog relevance or mechanism.
- A neural space preserves arbitrary exact original histories or scientific meaning.
- Rejected gap/resolution inputs establish accuracy under missingness or resampling.
- Source files being physically present establishes rights or approved support mapping.
- A 90% empirical interval achieves universal coverage for dependent prefixes/real data.
- Stored embeddings outperform cached complete predictions for identical requests.
- A local three-query materialization crossover establishes complete lifecycle benefit.
- Training/validation/test prefixes or repeated seeds are independent scientific fires.
- A convenience synthetic cohort establishes population significance or a universal
  representation decision boundary.

Track A requires verified reachable no-charge inference and actual frozen smoke/pilot.
Real Track B needs reviewed rights/attribution/support/availability and a bounded,
hash-verified source cohort with independent outcomes and fire/year/region splitting.
These gaps remain explicit; no mocks or synthetic labels fill them silently.
