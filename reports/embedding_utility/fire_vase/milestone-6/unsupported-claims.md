# Unsupported real-data claims

- New real observation rows were inspected, a real adapter executed, or real values
  were independently validated in M6.
- Local possession, current file sizes or historical hashes establish fresh byte
  identity, scientific compatibility, source-use rights or permission to redistribute.
- MIT software or the paper's CC BY license establishes the cached dataset's license.
- All same-named cumulative/ring/day variables have equivalent support or meaning.
- An absent day is zero, any adjacent rows are daily transitions, or unknown baseline
  and incomplete event fragments support full-event cumulative histories.
- Custom native CRS is EPSG4326 because that entry exists in the GeoPackage.
- Climate flags establish burned-area QA or a measurement uncertainty distribution.
- Source native dates establish real availability at model issue time or operational
  forecasting validity.
- A retrospective reconstruction satisfies the prospective objective without an
  explicit scope decision.
- Generated validator cases establish empirical FIRED correctness or expand synthetic
  results into real scientific evidence.
- Historical upstream cohort/transition totals are newly measured M6 selection bias,
  real prospective cohort size or representative wildfire coverage.
- Real performance/calibration/transfer/retrieval or a real-workload cost advantage
  has been measured, or synthetic winners generalize to actual observations.
- The approval packet, a validation PASS, a management record or the old inert
  RealPrefixApproval grants execution, training or publication authority.

No real model training, no new synthetic benchmark, no source copying/download,
no observation redistribution and no real-agent claim. Later read, scientific
cohort/training and release scopes must remain separately reviewed.
