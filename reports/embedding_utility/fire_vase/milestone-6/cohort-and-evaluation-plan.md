# Conditional real cohort and evaluation protocol

**PLANNED AFTER REVIEW. No real cohort or models have been executed.** The initial
[review packet](approval-packet.md) requests only a bounded native-value audit.
Do not treat this plan or source catalog registration as execution permission.

## Cohort construction after approved adapter verification

Resolve source daily-bin support and prefix origin first. A valid example retains
original native IDs, dates and quality/uncertainty, has a reviewed consecutive observed
prefix and genuinely supported future days. No observed zero is converted to missing;
missing/duplicate/gappy/ambiguous support produces exclusion/abstention, not imputation.
Prefix windows must not cross native events or substitute catalog final area for a
baseline. Partial audit fragments do not establish event origin.

For prospective mode, every input must have documented actual availability by its
issue time. No synthetic next-midnight timestamps may be assigned. If the source
supports only retrospective reconstruction, propose and obtain explicit approval for
a distinct retrospective experiment/contract; do not claim the prospective objective
has been achieved. M4/M5 synthetic-only runners do not accept real rows automatically.

A uses the next supported calendar-day area increment; B/C/D/E require three complete
future calendar-day observations. Future values supply independent targets and never
normalization, features, selection preferences or a source of filled missing values.
Selection on future support must be disclosed as observation-process conditioning.

## Selection-bias ledger

Track every source catalog event, native daily record, cache-matched record, complete
prefix, available-at-issue prefix, target-supported window and split eligibility.
Use nonoverlapping primary exclusion reasons plus a multi-flag audit; do not count
one fire with several windows as several independent fires. Report missingness and
source-quality strata before complete-case filtering. Preserve source/cache unmatched
rows/categories; a small approval sample is not a coverage estimate.

## Scientific splits and strata

Freeze whole-fire membership before fitting any preprocessing, PCA, encoder or head.
Propose chronological train/selection/calibration/test blocks only after reviewing
actual coverage and cross-year events; partial edge years remain explicit. Geographic
holdouts require reviewed native region/footprint definitions; do not replace source
ecoregions with arbitrary longitude labels or silently mix region semantics. Hold
all windows of a crossing/long event in one fold and document temporal purging rules.

Report by prefix depth, supported horizon, source completeness/QA, region/year and
catalog fire-size strata. Final size and completed developmental stage may stratify
retrospective evaluation but cannot enter prospective predictors or normalization.
Depth-from-observed-origin is not percent of final duration. Split construction and
support/exclusion diagnostics may inspect metadata, never choose folds because of
held-out model performance. No source-specific ecology is inferred from a label.

## Baselines and transfer, only after a separate training approval

Start with training mean, persistence, optimized raw-prefix ridge and explicit
observed-prefix summaries. Fit PCA only on real training folds. Retain the existing
177-parameter TCN and 168-parameter tabular MLP where the reviewed input contract
supports them; do not train larger architectures. Tune only a bounded preregistered
hyperparameter set on a distinct real validation cohort. Synthetic settings are
initial candidates, not presumed optimal. Keep selection and calibration fires
separate and hold test data unchanged.

Repeat only temporally supported A–E tasks. Freeze A-only transfer versus additional
AD/E supervision, model/head capacity and task-specific maintenance costs. Retrieval
relevance uses supported future outcomes and excludes all same-fire candidate prefixes;
it is not ecological analog relevance. No fabricated future rows or favorable-task
selection. Pair model losses at the fire level and distinguish conditional empirical
intervals from training/refitting uncertainty and wildfire-population generalization.

## Observation-process uncertainty and computation

Report predictive error and calibration separately from area/burn-date uncertainty,
timezone/support ambiguity, QA limitations and observation selection. Source-error
models or temporal perturbation magnitudes require evidence/expert approval. Without
that evidence, observational uncertainty is unquantified, not zero. Rejecting gaps
is not an accuracy robustness result.

Measure source access by bounded batch/window, preprocessing, model training,
representation construction, complete storage/lineage, on-demand heads, stored
embeddings and cached complete predictions on identical and heterogeneous workloads.
Keep optimized raw rivals and identity-aware invalidation. Actual source sizes,
read volume, preparation and refresh are measured after authorization; current local
synthetic timings cannot become real-workload or production lifecycle results.
No raw/harmonized archive persistence or source redistribution is implied.
