# Scientific uncertainty

- The input deliberately contains the scalar targets under evaluation. This tests compression/reuse of scientific state, not discovery of unknown ecological labels or end-to-end native sequence learning.
- Native measurement error, source quality, daily detection completeness, operational availability and physical lifetime are unknown. Mathematical masks do not resolve these questions.
- Observed increment sums/cumulative paths do not fill missing dates or establish complete physical fire area. Date origin and general order are discarded upstream of neural encoding.
- Single-seed fixed-budget models bound this implementation, not the best attainable model class. Objective heads and supervision differ. A 32-feature nonlinear probe cannot certify irrecoverability.
- Test-fire bootstrap intervals are conditional on fitted models. Six coarse source-reported regions give weak group inference; all 50 adjusted p-values are 1.0. No scientific equivalence margin was supplied.
- Mathematical property similarity is the only retrieval relevance standard here. Balanced and population-weighted query estimands differ; full-ranking diagnostics use a smaller independent gallery.
- Prediction is retrospective on selected observable futures. Complete one-point histories have no observed future, not zero growth or known extinction. Pretraining sees complete training-fire states; direct controls see prefixes.
- Cost is local warm-batch measurement. Physical disk bytes, concurrent/remote serving, true source refresh and production task mixture are unknown. Million-query totals are scenarios. Float32 and float64 storage differ.
- Source-specific rights evidence is retained from earlier verification; internal investigator authorization is not a third-party publication/redistribution receipt. No derived artifact is automatically cleared for external release.
- The initial probe-intercept/access-NA defects were fixed and downstream evaluations rerun. Archived invalid results must never be mixed with final tables.
